Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play games published by NeonMX by Saroj Jena ("we", "us") on the Apple App Store, including Lumablast. The short version: we don't collect any personal information ourselves. Your game progress stays on your device. The only information that leaves your device is what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, uses to show ads.

1. Information that stays on your device

The games save your progress (scores, gems, unlocked ships, upgrades) and settings (music, sound, vibration) on your device only. We never receive this information. There are no accounts, sign-ins, chats or in-app purchases, and the games don't use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games are free and show ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google. When ads are requested or shown, Google's software in the game may collect:

InformationUsed for
Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow trackingShowing ads more relevant to you and measuring them across apps
An identifier for the app on your device, and device details (model, iOS version, language)Delivering ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ads
IP address, used to estimate your general (not precise) locationDelivering ads suitable for your country or region, fraud prevention
How you interact with ads (views, taps)Measuring ads, fraud prevention
Crash and performance information from the ad softwareKeeping ads working

Google acts as an independent controller of this information. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. All ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

3. Tracking and your choice

The first time you open the game on iPhone, we show a short screen explaining why we ask, and then iOS asks whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

  • If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier to show you ads that are more relevant to you.
  • If you don't allow tracking, your advertising identifier isn't shared, and you'll see non-personalised ads based on things like the game itself and your general region.

Your choice never changes how the game plays. You can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. For child Apple Accounts and players under 18, iOS keeps this setting off, so the game can't ask to track and only shows non-personalised ads.

4. Players in the UK, EU and EEA

Where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent message before personalised ads can be used. You can change your choices at any time from the game's Settings → Ad privacy. Personalised ads rely on your consent. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention rely on the legitimate interest in keeping the game free and working. Google may process information outside the UK and EEA under appropriate safeguards described in its privacy policy.

5. Children

The games are suitable for all ages and contain no violence beyond abstract shapes, no chat and no purchases. They are general-audience games and aren't directed specifically at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you're a parent and have a question, please contact us.

6. How long information is kept

We don't hold any information about you. Information on your device is kept until you delete the game. Google keeps information it collects according to its own policies.

7. Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information collected by Google, use My Ad Center or Google's privacy tools, and you can reset your advertising identifier or turn off tracking in iOS Settings. If you're in the UK and unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
