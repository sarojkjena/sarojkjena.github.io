Common questions

How many free quizzes are there?
Each game (Flags, Find the Flag, Capitals, Currency and Challenge) has 7 free quizzes for life. Learn is always free.

How do I unlock everything?
Buy the Explorer Pass once from any unlock screen. A grown-up answers a quick question first, then Apple's payment sheet opens.

I reinstalled the app or have a new iPhone or iPad.
Open any unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought the Explorer Pass with.

I can't hear any sounds.
Check the 🔊 button at the top of the Play or Learn screen, and make sure the iPhone's ring/silent switch is set to ring.

Does it need the internet?
No. The app works fully offline. Only purchases and restores need a connection.
