Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

All in One Geography Quiz is a geography learning app for children and families. We built it so that it collects no personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any information about you or your child to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Quiz progress, stars and settings (such as sound on or off).
  • How many free quizzes have been used in each game, and whether the Explorer Pass is unlocked. On iPhone and iPad this is kept in the device's secure Keychain.

This information never leaves the device, apart from being included in your own device backups.

Purchases

The Explorer Pass is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers those purchases.

Children

The app is designed for children. Purchases are behind a grown-up check, and the app has no links that take children out of the app.

Website version

The web version of the quiz works the same way and stores progress only in your browser. On devices without colour flag emoji (such as some Windows computers), it loads an emoji font from Google Fonts so flags display correctly. The iPhone and iPad app does not do this.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
